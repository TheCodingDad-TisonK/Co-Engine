# The showcase

A hall on Co Engine to build in with every pack prop: the 67 from Grow Co. and the 15 basics. It was built by a playtest that drove the Co Engine editor through its MCP tools, and everything placed lives in `src/00-layout.js`, so it is the same every time you open it.

Open this folder in the Co Engine editor (Open..., pick `examples/showcase`) and press Play.

## What stands where

- **The office** (west, on the blue carpet): the office desk with its PC and chair, the filing cabinet, the bookshelf, the notice board, the floor lamp, the water cooler, the weapon locker, the vault, a fern, the key hook and the staff roster on the partition.
- **The lounge** (middle, on the vinyl): the sofa and coffee table, the couch, the lounge table and armchairs, the magazine table, the bed, the dining set, the rug, the dresser, the bookshelf, the side table, plants, the arcade cabinet.
- **The stock room** (east): the supply rack, the storage racking, the storage shelf, the pot shelf, the curing shelf, the drying line, the scale table, jar boxes, crates, soil sacks, the bucket, the hose reel, the floor fan, the workbench, the trim and bag line, the stock cabinet, a bin.
- **The shop floor** (south half): two goods shelves, the cigarette cabinet, the drinks fridge, the vending machine, the lobby coffee machine, the coffee bar, the menu screen, the rope line, the tablet dock, the trophy cabinet, the merch stand, the hydroponics bay, the breeding bench, the grow tent, the basement hatch, lobby benches, the window seat, ferns and plants, and a counter of four crates carrying the till, the tip jar, the business cards and the desk bell (dropped onto it under physics).
- **The forecourt** (outside the doorway): the sliding gate and the control panel that opens and closes it, the boom barrier, lamp posts, fence sections, cones, a bench, a bin, a planter, a standing sign, a lever, a pallet, and a wall screen and a wall sign on the hall.

## Commands

In the editor's console: `night` and `day` set the clock.

## Notes

Grow Co.'s props bring their look, not the shop's logic: a shelf that fills from Grow Co.'s stock stands empty, a machine you use says what it is. The control panel, the gate, the barrier and the lever are the basics pack's and work.
